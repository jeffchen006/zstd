Performance
-----------
Fresh independent tests: five alternating old/new pairs, built-in -i5.
Changes below are median paired throughput ratios. Brackets are individual
pair minima/maxima, NOT confidence intervals. Full absolute speeds are in
SUPPLEMENT.txt and the summary JSON files.

Silesia L9 compression:
  Mac Apple Clang17: +3.62% [+3.42,+4.94]
  Mac GCC15:         +3.52% [+3.11,+5.96]
  EPYC Clang14:      +1.63% [-0.97,+2.20]
  EPYC GCC11:        +0.61% [-0.52,+1.45]

The Mac L9 gain reproduces with both compilers. Small mixed x86 changes do
not establish a broad x86 benefit. Additional confirmed/screened losses are
retained, including mixed L6 file results and decoder observations.

The principal concern is EPYC GCC11 random16MiB L9 compression:
  Candidate: -7.84% median [-10.74,-3.66], all five pairs negative.
  Same-binary random A/A: -0.35% median [-1.78,+1.37].
The separate fixed256MiB repeated-random counter diagnostic shows nearly
unchanged GCC instructions (+0.018%) and cycles (-0.17%). It uses a different
work size/schedule and cannot invalidate the in-memory observations, but it
does not establish an algorithmic cost increase. The cause remains unresolved.
Flat profiles sample both compression and decompression; their memmove/row
matcher proportions do not isolate compression cost or prove causality.

Clang14's original Silesia L13 screen loss did not reproduce consistently:
longer median -0.69%, range[-3.73,+24.43]. Mac Clang L5 likewise has a large
retained outlier (+87.18% in one pair) and supports no subtle gain/loss claim.
No sample was removed merely because it was an unfavorable result or outlier.

Limits and reproduction
----------------------
Mac desktop placement/frequency/ASLR are uncontrolled. EPYC is shared;
process affinity and CPU/SMT guards do not control shared cache/memory or
schedutil frequency/boost. Final Silesia A/A ranges reach several percent on
EPYC; random Clang A/A includes a +9.29% outlier. Profiles and fixed-work
counters are separate from adaptive in-memory timing. No alignment perturbation
was performed. No general architecture, compiler, decoder, streaming or
dictionary performance improvement is claimed.

SUPPLEMENT.txt has complete tables, losses, controls and exclusions. Raw
performance/guard records, correctness, hashes, compiler/build settings and
bounded static proof accompany it. REPRODUCE.txt and reproduce_supplement.py
provide a standalone recheck; its bounded smoke passed and its timings are
excluded. Initial Mac A/A overlaps transfer and is excluded from calibration.
Invalid historical fullbench41 attempts, non-intercepting analyzer setup,
earlier implicit-thread counters, rejected load samples and quarantined older
shared-load experiments do not support final claims. Existing evidence blobs
are preserved; the original experiment and this supplement are not pooled.
